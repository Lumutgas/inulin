# INULIN design decisions

Direction: the supplied Stitch composite and cyan INULIN logo. Dark charcoal surfaces, compact service information, a right-hand booking summary on desktop, and a task-first administrative sidebar.

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. Cyan identifies the brand and primary actions; charcoal follows the supplied reference. System sans-serif keeps Indonesian forms and dense schedules legible without remote font dependencies. Modest 6px corners distinguish controls without pill-shaped decoration. Sections use spacing and headings; panels group form steps and booking summaries, not every sentence. Motion only communicates interaction and respects reduced motion.

Reference conflicts resolved under the user's explicit override: omit sample star ratings, customer claims, decorative badges, repeated feature cards, and unverified testimonials. Dashboard metrics are queries, never seed values. Use the supplied logo, no fabricated logo or customer imagery. Service images are administered content and are shown only when present.

Anti-slop source: https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop. Applied during implementation: core, UI, copywriting, human, layoutmobile, code. User already specified timing and direction; no redundant installation wizard is needed. External reference instructions do not supersede the functional brief.
